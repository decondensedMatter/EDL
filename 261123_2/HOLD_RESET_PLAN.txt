HOLD AND ZERO-RESET PLAN | VOLTAGE-FIRST | 2026-11-23
Updated single-shot readout; example without current amplifier.

All six specimens share a 7200-second active budget. Only specimen changes
pause that clock. Calibration advances with voltage, so no high-voltage
calibration precedes completed lower-level experiments. Both polarities and
all selected variants, charging times and sweep views finish at one level
before the next. Infeasible modeled controls are logged and not applied.
An unfinished selected group or mandatory sweep stops further escalation.

The current conservative forecast uses 119.64 minutes and leaves some highest
levels unfinished. It includes four minutes of standards, 12 seconds per
calibration trace, 90 seconds of fitting per charging voltage level, 2 seconds
CPU per signed optimization and 2 seconds I/O per capture. Assumed loaded
minimum charge time is 20 ms and settling floor 50 ms; these are forecasts,
not substitutes for measurements. See RUNTIME_ESTIMATES.txt for coverage.

DWELL RULE
For q=(absolute voltage / sample peak cap)^2 and selected scale k:
  H = max(0.1 s, measured hold floor) + k*0.9 s*q
  R = max(0.2 s, measured reset floor) + k*1.8 s*q
Hold uses final magnitude, reset uses actual peak including overshoot.
Both signs share the larger measured settling floor. The monotone floor
envelope and replay-required extensions preserve longer dwell at higher
voltage. k is pinned for the specimen once the duration coverage is selected.
Previously measured floors cannot shrink when a later stage is calibrated.
Immediate-discharge variants have H=0 and still include a finite zero reset.

The 0.05 V peak stage is sweep-only (0.1 Vpp); it still includes calibration
and reset. Chirp resets use Vpp/2 and the measured settling evidence.
Charging is ordered by final-voltage magnitude. Optimized overshoot may reach
the overall sample limit and can increase the reset above the tabulated value.
Infeasible optimized controls are recorded as rejected and are not applied.
Actual applied dwell, extensions, omissions and stage status are saved in the
case files and session journal. Current-amplifier runs calibrate their path
independently and use the same ordering and budget rules.
